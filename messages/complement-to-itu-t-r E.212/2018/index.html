International Identification Plan for Public Networks and Subscriptions OB no. 1161

Note from TSB

Following the decisions reached at ITU-T Study Group 2 meeting in July 2018, Geneva, the Director of the TSB has assigned the Mobile Country Code (MCC) (Recommendation ITU-T E.212) 991 as a shared MCC for trial of a proposed new international telecommunication service.

Note from TSB

Identification codes for international non-commercial trials

Associated with shared mobile country code 991 (MCC), for trial, the two-digit trial mobile network code (MNC) “01” has been temporarily assigned until 14 January 2020 to World’s Global Telecom (WGT) for the trial to test “BAC-TVT (Global Mobile Bot-Assisted Conference with Text-to-Voice-to-Text PLMN/PSTN for People with Disabilities)”. The temporary assignment of the E.212 shared MCC and trial-MNC 991 01 will be effective on 15 January 2019.
