International Identification Plan for Public Networks and Subscriptions OB no. 992

Serbia and Montenegro 

Joint Communiqué of 3.X.2011:

E.212 Mobile Country Code (MCC)

The Ministry of Culture, Media and Information Society, Belgrade , and the Ministry of Information Society and Telecommunications , Podgorica announce in this joint communiqué that they have signed an Agreement on the duration of the transition period of common usage of Mobile Country Code (MCC), as follows:

For Montenegro, both Mobile Country Codes (220 and 297) will be used, not later than 31 December 2011. From 1st January 2012, for Montenegro Mobile Country Code 297 will be used, only
