Finland +358

Communication of 19.V.2016

The Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority (FICORA) , Helsinki, announces the following information about the national numbering plan in Finland:

There appears to be uncertainty about termination rates to Finland's telephone numbering plan range +358 71, as some operators block the traffic to this number range. However, this number range is not associated with premium rate numbers.

The Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority (FICORA) wishes to inform that termination rates to number range

+358 71 abc def (gh) equal to termination rates to fixed line local numbers, such as number ranges beginning with

+358 13, +358 14, +358 15, +358 16, +358 17, +358 18, +358 19, +358 2, +358 3, +358 5X (X ≠ 0), +358 6, +358 8 and +358 9.

FICORA encourages international operators to reopen the traffic to numbers beginning with +358 71.
