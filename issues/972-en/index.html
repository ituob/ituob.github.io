Argentina

Communication of 16.XII.2010:

The Correo Oficial de La Republica Argentina S.A ., Buenos Aires, announces that from 1 February 2011, the telegrams indicated as TLXx (TELEX DELIVERY) will not be accepted anymore. This restriction will reach incoming and outgoing telegrams from the Argentine (Republic).

The special services such as URGENT (urgent transmission and delivery) TFx (telephone delivery), FAXx (facsimile delivery) will be available.
