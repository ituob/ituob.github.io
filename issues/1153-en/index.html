Thailand +66

Communication of 11.VII.2018

The Office of the National Broadcasting and Telecommunication Commission (the Office of NBTC), Bangkok, would like to inform that the National Broadcasting and Telecommunication Commission (NBTC) has approved a new numbering plan, which changes fixed line numbering structure from 9 to 10 digits, in order to increase the supply of fixed line and mobile numbers. This new numbering plan will be fully implemented in 2021. The time schedule agreed by all stakeholders is shown as follows:

1.   In January 1 st 2021, all Thai telecommunications operators must be ready to provide their services under the new numbering plan.

2.   By the second quarter of 2020, all fixed line telecommunications operators must provide 10 digit fixed line services in parallel with 9 digit service, and all mobile telecommunications operators must prepare their network system to provide services on new mobile categories.

In this regards, Thailand's new national numbering plan as per Rec. ITU-T E.129 format presentation will follow later in the process.

Uganda

Communication of 17.VII.2018:

The Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) , Kampala, announces a change in the national regulatory framework of Uganda.

Notification to network operators to ensure implementation of Recommendations ITU-T E.157 and ITU-T E.164

Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) has directed all operators in Uganda to establish call line identification (CLI) facilitates in line with the provisions of the ITU-T E.157 and ITU-T E.164 Recommendations.

Effective 1 st August 2018, the operators have been required to prevent any call with invalid or non-dialable CLI data from being connected to the called party in Uganda. This is to apply to both local and international calls.
