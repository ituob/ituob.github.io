Change in the Telegram Service

Croatia

Communication of 16.VII.2013:

The Croatian Post, Zagreb, announces that for telegrams addressed to recipients within Republic of Croatia, URGENT is cancelled.

Croatian Post secures transfer and delivery time of telegrams and mandates addressed for narrower delivery area of postal office with following deadlines:

a)          In post offices with organized telegram delivery

              –     Telegrams collected until 16:00 hours (Monday-Friday) and till 13:00 (Saturday) will be delivered during same day in Zagreb, Split, Rijeka, Osijek, Dubrovnik, Zadar and Pula

    Telegrams collected after these deadlines in mentioned post offices will be delivered on next working day

              –     In other post offices with organized telegram delivery, telegrams collected until 13:00 (Monday‑Friday) will be delivered on the same day

Telegrams collected after this deadline will be delivered on the next working day

b)          In post offices without organized telegram delivery, telegrams are delivered by regular postal delivery

              –     Telegrams collected in destination post offices before departure of postmen to their routes will be delivered on the same day and those collected after will be delivered on the next day

For any further information concerning the telegram service in Croatia, please contact:
