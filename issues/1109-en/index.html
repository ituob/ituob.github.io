Chile +56

Communication of 8.IX.2016

The Subsecretaría de Telecomunicaciones , Santiago de Chile, announces that as from 5 September 2016,  fixed-mobile portability will apply in Chile, and both fixed and mobile telephone subscriber numbering will therefore have the following structure:

+56 YXX XXX XXX

wherein:             Y may be any digit from 2 to 9;                            X may be any digit from 0 to 9.
