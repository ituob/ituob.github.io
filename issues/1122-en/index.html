Bosnia and Herzegovina +387

Communication of 21.III.2017

The Communications Regulatory Agency (RAK), Sarajevo, announces the Numbering plan for telephone services in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

It shall apply from: 1 st October 2017.

General information

  • E.164 Numbering Plan for telephone services in Bosnia and Herzegovina is presented according to the Recommendation ITU-T E.129.

  • Country code (CC): +387

  • International prefix: "00"

  • National prefix: "0"

  • NDC = National destination code

  • N(S)N = National (significant) number {NDC + SN}

  • SN = Subscriber Number

  • Format of international numbers: CC + NDC + SN

For all national calls, except for calls to short numbers, the National prefix must be dialled before N(S)N. It must not be dialled from abroad.
