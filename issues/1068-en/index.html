Hong Kong, China

Communication of 12.XI.2014:

Cessation of telex service

The Office of the Communications Authority (OFCA) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People’s Republic of China , Hong Kong, China, announces that, after 31 December 2014, all international and national telex service is no longer provided to and from PCCW Global Limited (“PCCW Global”) network in Hong Kong, China.

The Telex Destination Code (TDC) "802" allocated to Hong Kong, China (Recommendation ITU-T F.69), including the Telex Network Identification Code (TNIC) "HX" (Recommendation ITU-T F.68) (also used as telegram destination indicator), remain nevertheless reserved for Hong Kong, China.

For further information, please contact:

Mr Yim Chi-ho Office of the Communications Authority (OFCA) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People’s Republic of China 29/F, Wu Chung House 213 Queen’s Road East WANCHAI Hong Kong, China Fax:         +852 2803 5110 E-mail:    chiho-yim@ofca.gov.hk
