Austria

Communication of 5.I.2021:

On the occasion of the UEFA EURO 2020 (2021), the Austrian Administration authorizes an Austrian amateur station to use the special call signs OE2021EURO and OE21EURO from 7 June to 11 July 2021.

 

Communication of 7.I.2021:

On the occasion of the 18 th anniversary of the "Antarctic Activity Week", the Austrian Administration authorizes an Austrian amateur station to use the special call signs OE89ANT and OE90AAW from 20 to 28 February 2021.

On the occasion of the "100 jahre Burgenland bei Österreich", the Austrian Administration authorizes an Austrian amateur station to use the special call signs OE100BL and OE100VSW from 1 January to 31 December 2021.

On the occasion of the "100 jahre Burgenland bei Österreich", the Austrian Administration authorizes an Austrian amateur station to use the special call sign OE100LHP from 4 January to 31 December 2021.

On the occasion of the "100 jahre Burgenland bei Österreich", the Austrian Administration authorizes an Austrian amateur station to use the special call sign OE100POP from 15 January to 31 December 2021.

Communication of 14.I.2021:

On the occasion of the 165 years of Nikola Tesla's birth, the Serbian Administration authorizes radio stations of the Amateur Radio Union "ScwC - Serbian CW Club" to use the special call sign YT165TESLA from 1 January to 31 December 2021.
