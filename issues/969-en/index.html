Estonia +372

Communication of 2.XI.2010

The Estonian Technical Surveillance Authority (ETSA) , Tallinn, announces the updating (Regulation No. 55 of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications, 31 August 2010) of the Estonian National Numbering Plan (NNP) (Regulation No. 45 of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications, 11 June 2007).

General changes of the numbering plan are as follows:

Numbering range 40 is open for e-fax numbers (40XX XXXX) for the provision of e-Fax (eFax) service.

Numbering ranges 701 and 702 are open for the booking eight-digit personal numbers for the provision of Personal Communications Service.

Numbering range 705 is open for the booking of eight-digit mass calling numbers for the provision of Mass Calling Service.

From 1 January 2011 present (old) seven-digit numbers (70XXXXX) of the numbering range 70 used for the provision of Personal Communications Service will alter to eight-digit numbers (70 0X XXXX).  New free eight-digit numbers in the numbering range 700 shall be booked from 1 January 2013.

Calling to Estonia from abroad does not change.

When dialling from abroad to Estonia, after the international access code (international prefix) and the country code of Estonia (+372), the national (significant) number will follow. 

The maximum length of an international number is fifteen digits.
