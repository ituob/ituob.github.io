Brazil +55

Communication of 26.XI.2014

The Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações (ANATEL) , Brasilia, announces that the Numbering Plan of mobile services in Brazil will be modified due to the increasing demand for new numbers.

The current format of mobile numbers is N 8 N 7 N 6 N 5 N 4 N 3 N 2 N 1 and the first digit which identifies this service is 9, 8, 7 or 6. However, in accordance with Resolution No. 553, issued by the Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações (ANATEL) on 14 December 2010, there will be the inclusion of a ninth digit in mobile number length and all subscriber numbers will initiate with the digit “9”.

Therefore, mobile numbers will have the following format: +55 XX 9 XXXX XXXX (country code, area code and subscriber number).

This change was already implemented in the Area Codes 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 19 (State of São Paulo), in the Area Codes 21, 22, 24, 27 and 28 (States of Rio de Janeiro and Espírito Santo) and in the Area Codes 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 96, 97, 98 and 99 (States of Amapá, Amazonas, Maranhão, Pará and Roraima).

Now, in this year of 2015, the changing will occur in the Area Codes 81, 82, 83, 84, 85, 86, 87, 88, and 89 (States of Alagoas, Ceará, Paraiba, Pernambuco, Piauí, Rio Grande do Norte), on May 31 st ,   and in the Area Codes 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 37 and 38 (State of Minas Gerais) and 71, 73, 74, 75, 77 and 79 (States of Bahia and Sergipe), on October 11 th .

ANATEL will define in the future the date in which this change will take place in the other Area Codes of Brazil.

In addition, ANATEL would like to highlight that this format change will not be applied to mobile radio service numbers nor to fixed switched telephone numbers. Consequently, subscriber numbers for these services will remain eight-digit long.
