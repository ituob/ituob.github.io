Note from TSB

Identification codes for international non-commercial trials

Following the completion of the trial by World’s Global Telecom (WGT), TSB informs of the expiration of the assignment to WGT of trial numbering resources E.164.2 CC +991 with TIC 001 and E.212 MCC 991 with MNC 01.

The assignee has duly discontinued the use of the trial resources and has returned them to TSB at the end of the assignment period, 15 January 2021. The returned trial numbering resources E.164.2 CC +991 with TIC 001 and E.212 MCC 991 with MNC 01 are at disposal of the TSB Director.
