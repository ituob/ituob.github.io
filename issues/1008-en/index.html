In order to call a subscriber to the national fixed or mobile telecommunication networks (local or national call), a number of format SABPQMCDU is used.

III)         Calls to emergency services

The numbers used to contact one of the emergency services (two or three digits) from within the country take the following format:

1X, in which:      X = 8 (fire service) or X = 7 (police)

These services cannot be called from outside the country.
