Togo +228

Communication of 10.VIII.2011

The Autorité de Réglementation des secteurs de Postes et Télécommunications  recalls, Lomé , that as from 7 August 2011 at 00:00 hours UTC, numbering in Togo changed to eight (8) digits.

Fixed telephony

Operator:            Togo Télécom

Following the country code (+228) add digit 2 followed by your correspondent’s number.

Example               +228  221 4401 becomes +228 2221 4401

Former seven (7) digit numbers for Togo Télécom commence with: 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 32, 33, 44, 55, 66 and 77.

Mobile telephony

Operator:            Togo Cellulaire

a)          For numbers commencing with digit 9

              Following the country code (+228) replace digit 9 by 90; the rest remains unchanged.

              Example:             +228 9 010 001 becomes +228 90 010 001

b)          For numbers commencing with digit 0 and 8

              Following the country code (+228) replace digit 0 or 8 by 91.

              Example:             +228 8 345 620 becomes +228 91 345 620                                               +228 0 345 620  becomes +228 91 345 620

c)           For numbers commencing with digit 7

              Following the country code (+228) replace digit 7 by 92

              Example:             +228 7 620 401 becomes +228 92 620 401

Former seven (7) digit numbers for Togo Cellulaire commence with: 04, 06, 07, 08, 09, 72, 73, 74, 75, 76, 81, 82, 83, 85, 90, 91, 92, 93, 97, 98 and 99.

Operator:            Atlantique Telecom Togo

a)          For numbers commencing with digit 0, 6 and 8 :

              Following the country code (+228) replace digit 0, 6 or 8 by 98

              Example:             +228 0 113 040 becomes +228 98 113 040                                               +228 6 022 220 becomes +228 98 022 220                                               +228 8 452 080 becomes +228 98 452 080

b)          For numbers commencing with digit 5, 7 and 9

              Following the country code (+228) replace digit 5, 7 or 9 by 99

              Example:             +228 5 213 040 becomes +228 99 213 040                                               +228 7 011 080 becomes +228 99 011 080                                               +228 9 452 080 becomes +228 99 452 080

Former seven (7) digit numbers for Atlantique Telecom Togo commence with: 01, 02, 03, 05, 52, 53, 60, 70, 71, 84, 86, 87, 94, 95 and 96.
