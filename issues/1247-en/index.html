Communication of 25.V.2022:

On the occasion of the 25 th anniversary of the return of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to the motherland, the People’s Republic of China Administration authorizes an amateur radio operator in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to use a special call sign prefixed with " VR25 " from 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2023. Communication of 15.VI.2022:
