Other communications

Serbia

Communications of 14.X.2015:

On the occasion of the 100th anniversary of the Serbian army's retreat through Albania (the Albanian Golgotha, 1915-1916), the Republic of Serbia authorizes a number of Serbian amateur radio stations to use the special call sign YT100SG from 26 November 2015 to 15 February 2016.

On the occasion of 270 years since the arrival of the Slovaks in Bački Petrovac, the Republic of Serbia authorizes a number of Serbian amateur radio stations to use the special call sign YU270AJM from 1 November to 31 December 2015.
