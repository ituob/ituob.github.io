How to Add Service Restriction messages

This shows how add Service Restrictions message to an OB Edition.

  1. On the left side of the Issue Editor window under “General Messages”, click the circular plus button and select Service Restrictions.

  2. In message editor, click the circular plus button to add each Service Restriction item.

    Country name should be a string, while OB Edition and page are numbers.

  3. To remove an incorrectly added Service Restriction item, click the red cross button on the right.
